Fortis Wallet 0.5.1 is out.

A non-custodial Android wallet for Bitcoin XBT and Bitcoin BTC, locked with your fingerprint. Your recovery phrase is generated on your phone, encrypted there, and never leaves it.

New: swap BTC ⇄ XBT directly with another person. Both sides lock coins in an on-chain contract: either you both get paid, or you both get your coins back. Nobody holds your coins in the middle — not the other person, and not us.

Open source. On Zapstore:
