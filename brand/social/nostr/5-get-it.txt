Three ways to get Fortis Wallet on Android (8 and up):

• Zapstore: https://zapstore.dev/apps/com.fortistechlabs.wallet
• Obtainium, straight from the GitHub releases
• The APK: https://github.com/fortistechlabs/fortis/releases/latest

Every release is signed with the same key. Its certificate SHA-256 starts 161ab93f…cd48 — Android won't install an update signed by any other.
