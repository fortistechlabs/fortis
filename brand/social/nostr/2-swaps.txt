How a person-to-person swap works in Fortis Wallet:

1. Post an offer, take one from the list, or share a code with someone you know.
2. Whoever sends XBT goes first: the app locks it in a contract on chain.
3. The other side locks their BTC.
4. Each side claims the other's coins — claiming reveals the key that lets the other side claim theirs.

If someone backs out, the coins go back. The contract decides, not a person, and not us.

0.5% of what you send (at least 400 sat), plus the network fee. Start small — swaps are new.
