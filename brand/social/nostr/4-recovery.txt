Lost your phone halfway through a swap?

Restore your recovery phrase on a new phone, then Settings → Find my swaps. Fortis finds the swaps that still have coins locked and finishes them — collecting what you're owed, or refunding you.

And if the other side disappears, your coins come back on their own at the refund time. Their app normally releases them within minutes.
