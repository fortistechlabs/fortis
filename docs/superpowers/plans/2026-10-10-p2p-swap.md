# P2P BTC ⇄ XBT Swaps Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let two fortis users swap BTC ⇄ XBT with each other by HTLC atomic swap. The edge does matching, prices from NeoxEX, relays messages and runs a watchtower, and takes 0.5% per side.

**Architecture:**
- **wallet-core** stays pure. It gains:
  - agreed timelocks;
  - `SwapTerms` with validation;
  - a seedless `SwapKey`;
  - pre-signed spends at two feerates;
  - a serde-able `SwapMachine`.
- **wallet-ffi** wraps one swap as a `SwapSession`.
- **fortis-index** gains one `gettxout` route.
- **fortis-edge** gains a `swap/` module: NeoxEX price, a RocksDB store, matcher, relay, watchtower and routes, behind `--swap`.
- **Android** gains:
  - an API client;
  - a store with Keystore-wrapped per-swap keys;
  - an idempotent `SwapEngine`;
  - a foreground service plus a WorkManager fallback;
  - `SwapScreens.kt`.

**Tech Stack:**
- Rust: bitcoin, serde, rocksdb, tiny_http, ureq.
- Kotlin: Compose, OkHttp, WorkManager, Android Keystore.
- UniFFI via Gobley.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-p2p-swap-design.md`. The spec is approved and includes its "Revisions during planning" section.

## Global Constraints

- **No commits.** The owner commits everything. Wherever a task would commit, stop at a green suite instead.
- **Roles:** the XBT sender is always `SwapRole::Initiator`; the BTC sender is the `Participant`.
- **Price:** NeoxEX `BTCB2_BTC`, as the depth-weighted mid over the top ~0.05 BTC of each side. Refuse to quote when:
  - the spread is > 2%;
  - the data is > 60 s old;
  - the pair is paused;
  - the mid is > 2% from the last trade.
- **Price unit everywhere:** `price_sat_per_xbt: u64`, the BTC sats paid per 1 XBT (100 000 000 XBT sats).
  - `btc_sat = xbt_sat × price / 1e8`, computed in u128 and rounded down.
- **Commission:**
  - 50 bps per side, on the chain each user sends.
  - It is an extra output to the `GET /pricing` address, on top of the HTLC amount.
  - Built with `ServiceFee { bps: 50, floor_sat: 0, cap_sat: 0, fee_spk }`.
- **Minimum:** BTC leg ≥ 80 000 sat.
- **Cap:** BTC leg ≤ `--swap-max-sat`, default 1 000 000.
- **Locktimes** (absolute unix seconds, measured from `matched_at`):
  - XBT leg = `xbt.htlc_initiator_locktime_secs` (144 h mainnet);
  - BTC leg = `btc.htlc_participant_locktime_secs` (24 h).
  - Validation tolerance: ±600 s.
- **Confirmations:** XBT 6, BTC 2.
- **Deadlines:**
  - accept within 120 s of `matched_at`;
  - the initiator funds within 1800 s of `accept_by`;
  - the participant funds within 7200 s of the XBT funding reaching 6 confirmations, and never with < 12 h left on the BTC leg.
- **Matching:**
  - Offer expiry is 24 h by default.
  - Slippage choices are 50, 100 and 200 bps.
  - Matching is FIFO with partial fills; a remainder stays open only if it is ≥ the minimum.
- **Anti-griefing:** 3 missed funding deadlines in 24 h by one install token → no new offers for 24 h.
- **Store:** settled or expired records are purged after 7 days.
- **Watchtower:** ticks every 60 s.
- **Phone engine:**
  - every 20 s in the foreground service;
  - every 15 min in the worker, and on boot.
- **Edge flags:**
  - `--swap` (off by default; off → `/swap/*` returns 404);
  - `--swap-db <dir>`;
  - `--swap-fee-bps 50`;
  - `--swap-max-sat 1000000`;
  - `--neoxex-url https://neoxa.exchange`.
- **Strings:** English only.
- **Phase 1 limits:**
  - swaps use seed wallets only (no watch-only or hardware);
  - no wasm bindings.
- **New Android permissions:** `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`.
  - The plan adds `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` because the spec's "on boot" needs it.
- **Never install over the store app.** On a device, install and test only `com.fortistechlabs.wallet.dev`, never `com.fortistechlabs.wallet`.

## Review Focus

1. **The two legs of one match must build byte-identical HTLC scripts on both phones**: same locktimes, hashlock, and pubkeys in the right slots. Expected: the participant's `verify_their_funding` accepts the initiator's real funding tx. Pinned by `both_roles_build_the_same_contracts` (Task 2).
2. **A transaction that spends one swap HTLC input plus one ordinary wallet input must still pay the fee.** Pinned by `exemption_needs_every_input_to_be_a_swap_htlc` (Task 7).
3. **Process death between broadcasting the funding and saving its txid.** Expected: on restart, the engine finds the funding on chain (HTLC address funded) instead of funding twice. Pinned by `resume_after_funding_broadcast_does_not_fund_twice` (Task 10).
4. **The NeoxEX price moves between match and accept.** Expected: a phone whose own price is out of slippage declines; the match dissolves and both offers reopen. Pinned by `decline_reopens_both_offers` (Task 6) and `slippage_rejection_declines` (Task 10).
5. **The counterparty's funding tx pays the HTLC address but 1 sat short, or to a different locktime.** Expected: it is rejected. Pinned by `short_or_wrong_script_funding_is_rejected` (Task 3).

**Interfaces:**

  - `pub struct Ticker { pub last: f64, pub paused: bool }`

    - `NeoxEx::new(base_url: String) -> Self`

- **The depth-weighted mid:** for each side, walk levels from best price, adding `size × price` BTC until `DEPTH_BTC` is reached (truncating the last level). The side's price is the volume-weighted average of what was taken. Mid = (bid_vwap + ask_vwap) / 2. Spread = (ask_vwap − bid_vwap) / mid. The result is `round(mid × 1e8)` sat.
